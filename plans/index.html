and this second side will have one

do nightmare cause i don't wanna associate
this album with any negativity like that

mixtape

that wasn't in any other category and it was
like a hundred bucks, lmao.
it wasn't on streaming but it was on youtube,
that's how i found out it was a mixtape

that made me realize that i can get all the freedom
i want if i make a mixtape, especially since there's
no royalties stopping you from using whatever samples
you want

my mom always plays old music that i haven't heard
before, but it's usually so good and it's always
giving me ideas of what i could do with it.
so i definitely wanna make a mixtape, not sure how
it's gonna go, i just know i'm gonna do whatever
i want with it

cover ep

there's a youtube channel full of music edits
i've made, the reason i made them was because i
tend to hear stuff in music that makes me think
"hmm this could've been in the song" so i do it
myself. i even have it saved on my phone so i
can listen when i want to, sometimes i play it
more than the original lol.

as time progresses i've grown to think of what
some of my favorite songs would sound like
if they were sung by a different singer, played
on a different instrument, or composed in a
different genre. so once i've released an album,
i wanna have some of my favorite singers cover
some of my favorite songs with me on a ep.
no original songs, just covers. i think that
idea combined with the beautiful instrumentals
i have in mind, i feel like a lot of people
would appreciate me for making this. but either
way, the only thing i really care the most about
is working on each song until i like it, that's it.
